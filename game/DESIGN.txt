Portrait Godot 4.5.1 Compatibility, 540x960 logical canvas. Tiny Farm 16px art is

The core decision is scarce-water allocation to a specific harvest order. A bed
marked + was included in the authored order; hollow-circle beds are optional and
can substitute when their crop type is requested, but also consume precious water.
Channel drawing, erasing, undo, reset, retries and normal progression are free.

Simulation: channels form an undirected orthogonal graph. Rocks and closed gates
block traversal. Each connected tank component pools its supply exactly once.
Reachable beds drink by shortest distance from the nearest tank, ties by cell index
(top-left to bottom-right). A partially watered bed spends its available water but
does not harvest. Channels themselves do not consume water. Used + reserve = supply
for every component and every pulse. This is deterministic graph allocation, not
fluid physics. The UI shows costs before release and consumption after release.

100 stored distinct arrangements span ten ten-plot chapters. Two early tutorials,
then rocks, wider crop orders, gates and two-tank puzzles appear gradually. Every
stored witness is checked by the exact production simulate() method. First-clear
and improved-star rewards are idempotent; replays never farm the same reward.

Stars: deliver the order; retain the visible reserve target; use no more than the
authored channel target. A sponsored route hint marks the entire attempt assisted,
including undo/reset/save/load. Only unassisted three-star attempts count toward
the efficiency board. Earned callbacks are nonce-checked, single-use and scoped
to the current attempt. No native ad availability means no reward or fake benefit.

Grower XP/level with one-time level coin bonuses, four cosmetic palettes, daily
curated plot/quests and seven-day login coins. UTC date rollback does not reopen
claimed rewards. Daily completion does not bypass campaign unlocks. Local save uses
temporary replacement plus a previous-save backup, bounded loading and draft plans.

18 symbolic achievements and 4 boards are declared in game/catalog.json. Optional
/root/Services is accessed defensively. Home content refits to the actual native
banner/safe-area reserve, using stage screen-transform units and a change signal;
active puzzles are never rebuilt by that signal. round_finished fires only at
successful result transitions. The shared
adapter supplies the deliberate every-two-round interstitial cadence. Services,
addons, export/CI/marketing and production backend configuration are coordinator
owned. Web keeps the full local game and does not simulate native ad completion.

and never submits service progress. --demo --runtime-smoke tests actual viewport
touch dispatch, a release-button click, animation, receipt and secondary screens.
